10 / Paid social and search logic

Creative first. Then amplify.

Make them look twice.

  • Short walkaround and inspection films led by “Show the work”.
  • Broad but local audiences around Dubai.
  • Test hooks and early attention before scaling any creative direction.
Video view rateCost per 1,000 reached3-second hold

Show them the proof.

  • Retarget video viewers and site visitors with inspection reports and comparisons.
  • Offer a free report before the visit.
  • Answer common worries in the creative itself.
Saves and sharesProfile visitsWhatsApp starts

Make the visit easy.

  • Google Search on high-intent model, finance and local terms.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp with the car name pre-filled.
  • A booking prompt in every reply.
Cost per enquiryEnquiry to visit rateTime to first reply

Set budgets and benchmarks after baseline testing. No spend, audience size or performance result is assumed.
